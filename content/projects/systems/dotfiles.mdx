## Changelog

### 05/12/26
- Added `nosleep` script in `.local/bin/` that toggles a `systemd-inhibit` lock blocking idle, sleep, and lid-switch suspend. `nosleep on` starts the inhibitor in the background and prints `nosleep=on`; `nosleep off` kills it and prints `nosleep=off`; `nosleep` (or `nosleep status`) reports current state. PID tracked in `$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR/nosleep.pid` so toggling works across shells.
- Updated `install.sh` to chmod `nosleep` alongside the other `.local/bin/` scripts.

### 05/10/26
- Added tabbing/tiling behavior `cycle-tab.py` and `focus-tile.py` (i3ipc helpers) so arrow keys jump straight to the next tile instead of cycling through tabs, and Tab / Shift-Tab cycle within the nearest tabbed/stacked container with wrap.
- Rebound `$mod+Left/Down/Up/Right` to `focus-tile.py` and `$mod+Tab` / `$mod+Shift+Tab` to `cycle-tab.py` in i3 config.
- Updated `install.sh` to chmod the new `.py` scripts and add `python-i3ipc` to the pacman dependency list.
